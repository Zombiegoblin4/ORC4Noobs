### Part 1: Patching the roblox.com URL!!!111 :D
-----
#### 1.1 look up your AppSettings xml or whetever

![location](https://github.com/Zombiegoblin4/ORC4Noobs/blob/main/images/Ontwerp%20zonder%20titel%20(2).png "location")

	<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
	<Settings>
		<ContentFolder>content</ContentFolder>
		<BaseUrl>http://www.roblox.com</BaseUrl>
	</Settings>
